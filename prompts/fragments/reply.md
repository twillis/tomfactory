## How to end your reply

End your reply with one JSON object, and nothing after it, with exactly these keys. What
each one holds is said above.

```json
{{shape}}
```

You may also add one more key, `friction`: a list of short sentences saying what made this
step harder than it needed to be (something missing, unclear, contradictory or slow in what
you were given). It goes to the sprint's retro. Leave it out if nothing did.
