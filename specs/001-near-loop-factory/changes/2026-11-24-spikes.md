# Change record: spikes, a run whose product is an answer (2026-11-24)

Made outside spec kit, for spec kit to review later. Specification 004 (`specs/004-spikes/spec.md`), R1 to R12,
built as FR-113 to FR-118.

**Asked for by the operator.** "I wonder how we could fit r and d into this? Like as a lightweight spike." On the
outline: "Goals to deliver the simplest thing that gets the answer. You and I review against the plan." His
decisions of 2026-11-24: findings reach the main line through the orchestrator, not the factory (R6 amended); the
probe is run by a `probe_command` in the target's committed settings; a $5.00 cap per spike, settable per target.

**Changed**

- **FR-113, `tables.py`.** `SPIKE` and `SPIKE_STEPS`: `spike_plan`, `probe`, `run_probe`, `write_findings`,
  `spike_retro`, `finish`. No wait, no change-cycle gate, no smell. The plan and retro have prompts of their own, so
  their steps have names of their own (a prompt is found by its step's name). `workflow.py`: the scope `checkout`
  (anything but `PROTECTED`, `checks.py`). `operations.py`: `_can_spike` refuses, at the request, a codebase with no
  `probe_command`, one without `{path}`, or whose `docs_paths` do not hold `docs/spikes/`.
- **FR-114, `checks.py`.** `probe_ran`: runs `probe_command` contained, through the same setup-then-run path as the
  tests (`_set_up_then`, split out of `run_tests`). Cannot run: no such file in the checkout, setup failed, timed
  out, or an exit code in `probe_cannot_run_exit_codes` (`no_tests_exit_code` alone when it is empty; pytest exits
  2 on a collection error, so bttr_erp's will be `[2, 5]`, the orchestrator's ruling). `workflow.Rework.then` may be an `End`, so `run_probe` mends the probe once and
  then ends the spike inconclusive. `config.py`: `probe_command`, `probe_cannot_run_exit_codes`, `cannot_run_codes`, `probe_argv`. `driver.py`: inputs `probe_command`
  and `probe_output`.
- **FR-115, `checks.py`.** `findings_quoted`: the note is at `run_record.findings_note(run)`; each code block is a
  run of whole lines (up to a shared indentation) in a file on the branch the findings step did not write; each
  `text` block is in what the probe printed. Sent back once, then inconclusive. `workspace.py`: `on_branch`.
- **FR-116, `workflow.py`.** `FOUND` (answered, inconclusive) in `OUTCOMES`; `END_AS_FOUND`, `as_found` and
  `ends_as_found`; `next_step` takes what the findings said (`records.found`); `after_wait` ends an abandoned spike
  inconclusive. `store.py`: migration 5 widens `runs.outcome`. `run_record.py`: `Answered:` and `Inconclusive:`,
  the spike's plan in its record, and `not_in_the_codebase` in the record and the last commit.
- **FR-117, `workflow.py`.** `over_budget`, checked by the driver before every step from `records.so_far`.
  `config.py`: `spike_budget_dollars`, 5.0, 0 for none.
- **FR-118.** `personas/spike.md` (Juno Halloran, Prospector; a row in `docs/roster.md`); `prompts/spike_plan.md`,
  `probe.md`, `write_findings.md`, `spike_retro.md`, `run_kinds/spike.md`; driver inputs `branch` and
  `spike_answer`.

**Not in this change**

- R9's review and R10 are the orchestrator's: `orchestrator/playbook.md` says how. R10 needs nothing built: a
  request's reading list is its own text.
- R12's scoring: specification 003 has no scorecard yet to add a spike to.
- A spike ends needs-me where any run would (the agent program failing, a guard, a timeout of an agent step), so it
  can be resumed. A resumed spike at its budget ends again at once: the budget is read from the starting commit.
- The lead answering a question is not checked against the budget; it runs on the light grade.

Tests: `tests/test_spikes.py` (new, 31 cases); `tests/test_step_shape.py` (the `checkout` scope),
`tests/test_personas.py` (the spike's personas) and `tests/test_workflow.py` (the spike table's id) extended;
`tests/fake_agent.py` reports a `cost` when given one.
